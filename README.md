# Welcome to Verisult 👋

Empowering professionals through practical training, industry-recognized project management certifications, and real-world workplace experience. 

## 🌐 About Us
Verisult College (**verisultcollege.ca**) is a modern educational platform delivering structured online learning, dual-path student enrollment workflows, and career-focused training systems. We bridge the gap between education and execution by combining hands-on technical projects with industry-standard frameworks.

## 💎 What We Stand For: Our Core Values (EPIC)
* **Excellence:** Committed to delivering exceptional learning experiences, high-quality programs, strong learner support, and meaningful outcomes across our operations, partnerships, and service delivery.
* **Possibility Mentality:** Believing people are capable of more than they realize—helping learners see opportunities, recognize the value of their existing experience, and pursue growth with confidence.
* **Integrity:** Acting with honesty, transparency, accountability, and professionalism by always putting the best interests of our learners, partners, and stakeholders first.
* **Continuous Improvement:** Dedicated to learning, innovation, and growth to better serve learners, employers, and the communities we support.

## 🏆 Recognized & Trusted
* **PMI Authorized Training Partner:** Verisult is an Authorized Training Partner of the Project Management Institute (PMI).
* **Government Licensed:** Licensed by the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador, ensuring learners receive recognized certification.

## 🚀 Our Mission
To deliver scalable digital learning ecosystems that prepare learners for today’s workforce through real-world project execution and professional mentorship.
